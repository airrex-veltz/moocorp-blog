The boundary marker of Zone 11 was nothing. A single plastic buoy, one reflective strip glinting in the void. That was all. But every time Choi Min-jun saw that buoy, his heart tightened.

It had been two days. The mining vessel Danno 3 was circling 2 kilometers outside Zone 11's perimeter. To acquire more exploration data, they needed to enter inside the boundary. Entry required a license. The license had to be won at an auction opening in four days. To win the auction, they had to bid higher than Hwansung Corporation.

Sunwon Mining, which Choi Min-jun led, was uncertain whether it even possessed that number.

"Chief."

Yura, the communications officer, pulled off her headset as she spoke. Beneath her eyes lay seventy-two hours of fatigue.

"Another message from Hwansung. Frequency warning. They say we're within their exploration radius."

"Within their radius? We're outside the boundary."

"They seem to regard all of Zone 11 as within their radius."

Choi Min-jun closed his mouth. It was a signal that Hwansung Corporation had already taken control of the zone before even winning the license. It was they who had placed company satellites in low Earth orbit. They had accumulated exploration data before the auction announcement came out, and submitted reports to the regulatory agency estimating ore deposits per zone based on that data. It was legal. Sunwon Mining simply didn't have the capital to do the same.

"Ignore it."

"But—"

"Ignore them, Yura."

A thud came from the hangar direction. Choi Min-jun turned his body. O Ki-jun, the drill team leader, was running down the narrow corridor. Helmet in hand, his face half-open.

"Chief, the B-section drill head."

"What about it?"

"It's gone. The entire cooling system."

The cooling system. Choi Min-jun looked up at the ceiling once. If the drill head goes out, the ongoing perimeter exploration stops. If exploration stops, there's no supplementary data for the auction bid. Without supplementary data, Sunwon Mining's estimated ore deposit figure would be less than half of Hwansung Corporation's. Then there'd be no license, no contracts this season, no operating funds for next year.

He walked toward the aft porthole. The reflective strip of the Zone 11 boundary buoy was flickering in the distance.

In the early 2020s, two first-generation space mining companies closed their doors in succession. One had exhausted its investment capital six years after launching an asteroid exploration probe, and the other had completed the technology but was acquired before its first commercial mining operation. Both companies weren't wrong. It was just that capital burned faster than technology. Sunwon Mining was born from those ashes. Supply transport, satellite component delivery, small exploration support. They had survived for ten years that way. Not large, but alive.

"Do we have cooling parts in reserve?"

O Ki-jun shook his head.

"The reserves are at the station. Minimum thirty-six hours."

"The auction is in seventy-two hours."

"I know."

Both fell silent for a moment. The vacuum darkness waited beyond the window. The buoy flickered again in the distance.

"Contact the station. Priority shipping."

"The cost—"

"I'll handle it. Contact them now."

The moment O Ki-jun turned around, an alarm sounded. A low, intermittent tone. Yura tapped her screen.

"Approaching object. It's a Hwansung mining vessel—they're entering the boundary."

"Entering the boundary?"

"Yes. Inside Zone 11 boundary, coordinates Delta-7."

Choi Min-jun walked quickly to the instrument panel. On the screen was a trajectory showing Hwansung Corporation's small exploration probe crossing the boundary marker and entering inside the zone. It wasn't legal. No sample extraction activities were permitted inside the boundary without a license. But if the exploration probe was reported as simply for transit purposes, the regulatory agency couldn't intervene. It was a loophole in the regulations.

"Record it."

"What?"

"All coordinates, times, trajectories. Record everything. It might be useful later."

Yura stared at him for a moment, then nodded.

Zone 11 contained nickel and iron, and platinum-group elements at levels exceeding Sunwon Mining's own economic viability threshold. The exact figures didn't exist. It was an estimate. But based on estimate alone, three companies had interest in this zone, and Sunwon Mining was one of them.

The other two were incomparable in capital terms.

O Ki-jun appeared through the corridor again. This time he carried a toolbox.

"Priority shipping confirmed. Thirty-two hours."

"Not forty hours?"

"The station has something in progress, so they're shipping it together with that."

Choi Min-jun paused for a moment. Thirty-two hours meant the parts would arrive forty hours before the auction. Installing overnight would let them resume exploration. At least six hours of data. Whether that would be enough, he didn't know. But it was better than nothing.

"Understood. Get ready."

He walked back toward the aft porthole. The Zone 11 boundary buoy was still there. A small, seemingly worthless reflective strip, reflecting light without promising anything.

Seventy-two hours until the auction.

Probably thirty hours for drill head repair.

Forty hours left for data exploration.

The math didn't work. But this was how Sunwon Mining had survived for ten years. Reaching out where the numbers didn't add up.

Yura spoke. "The Hwansung exploration probe is still inside the zone."

"Keep recording."

"Yes."

The buoy flickered from across the vacuum. Once, and again.

Choi Min-jun watched it. He said nothing. Because nothing had ended yet.
