## The Great Cell Culture Operation of Songdo

A massive silver building in Songdo, Incheon. Inside, hundreds of millions of cells were being carefully cultured today as well. And the people caring for those cells were equally busy.

"This batch has to be perfect. Per-fect!"

Kim Min-seo, a 34-year-old researcher, looked at her team members in front of the clean room with the solemn expression of a general heading into battle. Her nickname was "Cell Mother." She cherished each monoclonal antibody cell line being cultured as if they were her own children, and would lose sleep if the temperature wavered even 0.2 degrees.

"Min-seo, let the cells breathe a little."

Next to her, Park Ji-hun, a 29-year-old engineer, chuckled while sipping his coffee. His nickname was "Songdo's Free Spirit." He executed equipment inspections flawlessly, but often conducted unexpected experiments in the process.

"Ji-hun, don't joke around. This is an important project we're delivering to a global pharmaceutical company!"

"I know, I know. That's why I proposed that new monitoring system. If we check the cell status 24/7 with AI, you might actually get some sleep at night..."

Min-seo shook her head firmly. "How can I trust a machine? Cells need to be observed directly with the eye."

That afternoon, a crisis erupted. The temperature in cultivation tank number 3 suddenly rose by 0.5 degrees. Min-seo rushed over screaming, while Ji-hun calmly followed with his tablet in hand.

"What do I do! The cells in tank 3...!"

"Calm down. My AI system already detected it. There's a microscopic air bubble in the cooling pump line. It's automatically correcting now."

Looking at the screen, the temperature was indeed returning to normal. Min-seo stared at Ji-hun with a blank expression.

"When... when did this start operating?"

"Oh, since last week? I installed it secretly, and since it was working well, I just left it. Your under-eye dark circles looked so bad..."

Min-seo didn't know what to say and closed her mouth, but then a laugh burst out.

"So Songdo's Free Spirit saved Songdo's Mother."

"Cell Mother."

"Right, Cell Mother."

That evening, the two of them took a walk through Songdo Central Park and talked about their future projects. A perfectionist and a free spirit. At first glance, an unlikely combination, but on the stage of the biotech industry, they created surprising synergy.

"By the way, Min-seo, is it true you've given names to the cells?"

"Well... tank 3 is 'Dandelion' and tank 7 is 'Sunflower.' Is that a problem?"

Ji-hun burst into laughter, holding his sides. "Truly the Cell Mother!"

In the night of Songdo, Incheon. The lights of the massive biotech facility sparkled. Inside, hundreds of millions of cells were—probably—still growing hard today. And the people protecting them too.
