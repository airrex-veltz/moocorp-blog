'Speed is everything.' Larry stared at the monitor. Summer 1998, Stanford University dormitory was sweltering from the server heat. Hard drives lined the walls hummed without ceasing.

Sergei stopped typing. 'Click Portal rejected our proposal. They said they'd buy our search engine technology for a million dollars.'

Larry laughed bitterly. 'Then we'll do it ourselves. They don't know what we've built.'

The search engine market was already saturated. Click Portal, Search Com, Explorer. Dozens of billion-dollar giants controlled the gates of the internet. But Larry and Sergei had a secret weapon. PageRank. A revolutionary algorithm that evaluated webpage importance by the quality of links pointing to it.

'The test results came back.' Sergei pointed at the screen. For the same search queries, Shin Search's results were 73% more accurate than Click Portal's. 'We find exactly what people are looking for.'

They rented Susan Wojcicki's garage. Rent was $1,700 a month. Server fan noise hummed through the night. Neighbors started filing noise complaints, but they never stopped.

In 1999, Shin Search logged 3.5 million searches a day. Venture capitalists came flocking. Sequoia and KPCB each invested $12.5 million.

Spring 2000, the dot-com bubble burst. Startups across Silicon Valley collapsed. Investors panicked and funding dried up.

'We need to completely change our advertising model.' Larry stood before the whiteboard. 'Banner ads are dead. We need a way to make money without damaging user experience. Show only ads related to the search.'

AdWords was born. Small text ads related to search results. Pay-per-click model. Simple, but revolutionary. Advertisers could measure effectiveness and users saw only relevant ads.

By 2002, Shin Search had broken $100 million in monthly revenue. Click Portal belatedly realized their mistake.

Windows Corp made its move. The CEO declared search a 'matter of survival.' They poured billions into Windows Search and began poaching Shin Search engineers.

'Microsoft is stealing our talent.' CEO Schmidt called an emergency executive meeting. 'They're offering double salaries.'

Larry replied calmly. 'Money alone won't work. We're selling a mission. A vision to organize all the world's information.'

August 19, 2004, Shin Search IPO. The stock started at $85 and climbed 18% on the first day, crossing $100. Market cap of $27 billion. Larry and Sergei became billionaires overnight, yet still showed up to work in jeans and t-shirts.

But the real war was only beginning.

In 2005, Shin Search acquired Mobile OS for $50 million. A mobile operating system startup of just twenty-two people that nobody paid attention to.

'Mobile is the future.' Larry declared at the board meeting. 'In ten years, everyone will be carrying a computer in their pocket. We need to own that OS.'

2007, Steve launched the smartphone. The world changed. 2008, Shin Search released Mobile OS 1.0. Market response was lukewarm. It looked like an inferior knockoff of the smartphone.

'We open source it.' Sergei announced at the developer conference. 'Mobile OS is free. Anyone can modify and distribute it. We're building an ecosystem.'

Sun Electronics, Shinra Electronics, HTC, Motorola jumped in. Hundreds of manufacturers started making Mobile OS phones. Two years later, Mobile OS overtook iOS in market share. Five years later it exceeded 80%.

Windows Corp admitted defeat in the search war. Shin Search's search market share exceeded 90% worldwide. Daily searches reached 6 billion.

In 2015, Shin Search reorganized as Shin Search Group. Search became just one division. Self-driving cars, biotech, AI research. Larry and Sergei prepared for the next war.

2023, ChatCore Chips Residence appeared. A new enemy. Not search, but conversation. Users started asking chatbots instead of Shin Search.

CEO Pichai declared 'Code Red'. A company-wide emergency. AI could replace search.

But Shin Search was already prepared. Alpha Intelligence from AI research, transformer architecture, language models. AI research assets accumulated over a decade.

Bard AI launched. Gemini AI followed. The war continued on a new front.

From a garage to ruling the world. Shin Search never stopped. And it was preparing victory on the next battlefield.
