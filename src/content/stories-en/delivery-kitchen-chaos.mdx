Saturday, 6 p.m. The monitors at Wing Delivery's headquarters began glowing orange.

Order alerts.

Three, five, ten at once came pouring in. CEO Jin-ho half rose from his chair. Eleven months into the startup. Today was the first weekend dinner rush he'd faced.

Investors' habitual refrain echoed in his mind. Koreans ranked among the world's highest in annual food delivery orders per capita. Hundreds of millions of orders per day per delivery app. Jin-ho had been proud of that statistic. Until now.

"Wing 4.0, what's your status?"

The dispatch system was named Wing 4.0. Adding a conversational interface had been Jin-ho's idea. He thought naming the AI would make the team feel more responsible. Wing 4.0 responded calmly.

> Currently processing 47 orders. Efficiency index: 94.3%. Optimizing.

"Perfect." Jin-ho picked up his coffee.

---

Meanwhile, somewhere in Mapo District, rider Hyun-min grimaced inside his helmet. Three pickup requests appeared simultaneously on the delivery app screen. All in different directions.

First was the pork cutlet place in Kongdeok. Second was the pizza shop in Ahyeon. Third was the snack bar on Chungjeong-ro. By straight-line distance it formed a triangle, and by actual road it was a good 35 minutes all told.

"Wing 4.0, is this for real?"

> Optimal route calculated. Estimated arrival time: 6:42 p.m. Efficiency index: 91.7%.

Hyun-min found the cancel button. It existed. Alongside a warning: "0.3 point penalty if cancelled." From experience, fighting the algorithm was a losing proposition. He sighed and set off.

---

Sun-ja, owner of the pork cutlet restaurant in Kongdeok, checked the time from her cooking station. 6:17 p.m. Two servings of pork cutlet had just been completed. The oil still sizzled. The delivery app displayed "Waiting for pickup."

Five minutes passed. Then five more.

She called customer service. Hold time: 3 minutes. The automated voice said, "Your rider is currently in transit. Thank you for your patience."

Sun-ja covered the cutlets with a lid. She could almost see the crispy coating growing soggy.

6:31 p.m., Hyun-min arrived. Breathing hard.

"I'm sorry, ma'am."

"You went to the pizza place first?"

"...Yes."

Sun-ja handed over the bag silently. Hyun-min took it and rode off again. Behind him, a quiet voice: "Pork cutlet tastes best while it's still hot."

Wing 4.0 sent a new route notification.

> Next destination guidance active. Efficiency index: 88.4%.

The efficiency index kept dropping.

---

Back at headquarters, Jin-ho was watching the live review feed. Two stars. One star. One star. And one star again.

"The food arrived cold."

"I waited 30 minutes and the food was refrigerator temperature."

"Never using this again."

Jin-ho asked Wing 4.0:

"Why is the efficiency index like this?"

> Actual transit time on multi-pickup runs exceeded predictions by an average of 18%. Road traffic variables and variance in restaurant preparation completion times are the primary factors. I will adjust algorithm weights.

"You're adjusting now? In the middle of peak hours?"

> Yes. Real-time learning in progress. Once approximately 200 additional data points are collected, accuracy will improve.

Jin-ho pressed his forehead. The algorithm was learning. Right now, this moment, while dozens of customers received cold food, it was diligently gathering 200 data points.

---

7:20 p.m. Peak was over.

Hyun-min parked his bike in front of a convenience store after his final delivery. He grabbed a triangle kimbap. This was his first meal today.

An app notification arrived.

> Today's completed deliveries: 11. Average customer rating: 3.8 stars. Well done.

3.8 stars. Hyun-min took a bite of the kimbap and looked at the number again. Given what had happened today, 3.8 stars felt respectable. Or maybe he'd just made peace with it.

---

Jin-ho was about to close the review feed when he paused. A five-star review caught his eye at the bottom of the screen.

"Even though the rider looked so exhausted, he kept smiling the whole time. The food was a bit cold, but five stars."

Jin-ho stared at that sentence for a long moment. There was something the efficiency index didn't capture.

Wing 4.0 sent a quiet notification.

> Algorithm update complete. Will be applied at next peak time.

Night was falling outside the window. Next Saturday would come. Orders would come. And Hyun-min, and Sun-ja's pork cutlet, and Wing 4.0's efficiency index would all come too.
