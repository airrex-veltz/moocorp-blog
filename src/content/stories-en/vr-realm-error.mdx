Zung Hyun Technologies showed up for work today. Well, he thought he showed up for work.

The moment he stepped into the building lobby, three potted plants were floating in mid-air. Or at least, they appeared to be. Zung Hyun Technologies tilted his head once, then quickly went numb to it. Ever since software version 3.14 rolled out last month, things like this had become far too common. Floating plants were actually the least of it.

The head of quality assurance came running down the hallway, shoving a tablet at him.

"Director, we've exceeded 870 customer complaints in just two days."

"What kind of complaints?"

"They're saying that when they wear the glasses, reality looks like augmented reality, and when they take them off, augmented reality looks like reality. One customer thought the subway entrance disappeared on the way to work and walked three stations."

Zung Hyun Technologies chewed on that for a moment. Then he slowly walked toward the window. Outside, the autumn leaves had turned. The foliage was red and yellow.

Is that real autumn, or is it a season the software layered on top?

Zung Hyun Technologies couldn't be sure.

---

Actually, the problem hadn't come without warning. The previous autumn, a competitor in the North American market updated their mixed reality headset firmware and caused a spatial calibration error, leaving several thousand users unable to distinguish between doorways and walls for four days. At the time, word around Zung Hyun Technologies was "that could never happen to us." Engineers sipped their coffee and laughed at the article.

Now those same engineers were pausing to check whether the office chairs they were about to sit in were real objects or renderings. One had started standing while he worked. Not because he didn't trust the chair, but because he couldn't trust his own doubt.

---

At eleven in the morning, an emergency meeting was called. The conference room whiteboard was covered with bug flowcharts. But in one corner of the diagram, a sticky note was stuck on.

"This flowchart itself may be an AR overlay."

The culprit was never identified.

The head of quality assurance began his presentation.

"The spatial awareness module in version 3.14 reversed the priority flag between the reality layer and the augmentation layer. In other words, the software learned the order of importance backwards."

"What's the solution?"

"All we have to do is flip the priority flag back."

"Then that's a quick fix."

"The problem is..." the team lead cleared his throat. "We're still determining whether that setting exists in the real UI or the augmented UI."

A long silence fell over the conference room. A pigeon flew past outside the window. Three people stared at the pigeon suspiciously.

---

At two in the afternoon, a bigger problem erupted. An investor video conference was scheduled. Zung Hyun Technologies had prepared a presentation while wearing his company's glasses, but the quarterly performance numbers displayed on the slides couldn't be distinguished between actual figures and numbers covered by the augmentation layer. The screen boldly displayed a 32 percent revenue growth rate. The previous quarter's performance was half that.

The person in charge silently lowered the slides.

The meeting was "postponed due to technical difficulties." It really was a technical difficulty.

---

At three-thirty in the afternoon, Zung Hyun Technologies called his company's customer service center. It felt strange to be asking his own company's product's problems to a customer service line his own company built, but there were no other options.

"Hello, this is Zung Hyun Technologies customer service. We have a high volume of calls at the moment. Please hold..."

Waiting music played. It was soft classical piano.

But Zung Hyun Technologies couldn't tell for a moment whether that music was the actual audio or interference from the augmented audio layer. According to the morning report, there was a possibility the bug had spread to the audio module as well. He looked out the window while holding the phone. The autumn leaves were still red and yellow. They swayed in the wind.

The physics engine doesn't implement trembling that fine. It's probably real autumn.

Probably.

---

Four days later, a junior developer found the bug. At the bottom of the config file, in a debug menu hardly anyone opened, was a single line: `realityPriority: false`. Someone had toggled it on during testing and deployed it as-is. The fix was a single character. Changing `false` to `true`.

As the patch was being deployed, the team lead added a comment.

"But... we did fix this correctly, right?"

No one answered. Instead, the developers took off their glasses one by one, put them back on, and looked out the window.

The autumn leaves were still there.
