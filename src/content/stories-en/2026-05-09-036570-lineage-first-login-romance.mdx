Autumn 1998, a small office in Gangnam, Seoul. The development studio at NC Soft remained lit well past 11 p.m.

"It crashed again."

Ji-woo tapped her monitor with a sigh. The Lineage beta test server had gone down once more. With launch just a week away, the server collapsed whenever concurrent users exceeded 1,000.

"Let me look."

Jun-ho pulled his chair over from the next desk. His monitor displayed dense C++ code. Jun-ho, a network engineer six months into the job, and Ji-woo, a game designer in her second year, were the two who stayed latest in the Lineage team.

"Here... the packet handling logic is tangled."

Jun-ho's finger traced the code. As Ji-woo leaned in to study the screen, her hair brushed against his shoulder. A faint shampoo scent. For a moment, Jun-ho's breath caught in his throat.

"...What did you say?"

"This part here."

Jun-ho continued his explanation in a flustered voice. But Ji-woo had already noticed his ears turning red. A smile played at her lips without her knowing it.

---

The two grew closer from then on.

As all-nighters piled up, Ji-woo and Jun-ho shared instant ramen before dawn, debugged together, and sometimes climbed to the rooftop to gaze at Seoul's nighttime cityscape through cigarette smoke—though Ji-woo never smoked, she went along with Jun-ho.

"Do you think this game will really work?" Ji-woo asked one night. Jun-ho shook his head.

"I don't know. But..."

He looked at Ji-woo.

"...the 'blood alliance' system you designed is truly remarkable. People will feel real bonds within the game. That's the heart of this game."

Ji-woo's heart raced. No one at the company had ever evaluated her design so seriously. Everyone else just asked, "Will it be fun?" or "Isn't it too complex?"

"Thank you."

She whispered.

---

September 3, 1998. The official launch day of Lineage.

At 5 a.m., the development studio was charged with tension. CEO Kim Taek-jin was personally monitoring the servers. The concurrent user count climbed slowly.

500... 1,000... 1,500...

"It's holding!" someone shouted. They'd exceeded 2,000 users. The server was still intact. Jun-ho's all-night optimization had paid off.

Ji-woo found Jun-ho. He was crouching beneath a desk, organizing cables. She tapped his back.

"We did it. We made it."

Jun-ho looked up. His eyes were bloodshot, but they gleamed with joy beyond belief.

"Really... really?"

"Yes. We've passed 3,000 concurrent users."

The two embraced right there. Amid the cheers around them, their own moment stretched on.

---

Six months later, Lineage rewrote Korean gaming history. 100,000 concurrent users. NC Soft rose from a venture startup to industry leader.

And one spring day, in a café near the office, Jun-ho handed Ji-woo a small box.

"What... is this?"

"Open it."

Inside the box lay a small silver ring. Inside the band, small letters were engraved.

**"First Login: 1998.09.03"**

"It's our first login," Jun-ho said.

"And... my heart too."

Tears welled in Ji-woo's eyes. She nodded.

The two began their story then, alongside Lineage. Like the blood alliance in the game, they became each other's most steadfast companion and lover in reality.

A romance born in an all-night studio. A love that bloomed between code and bugs. That was the first legend of NC Soft, and of Ji-woo and Jun-ho.
