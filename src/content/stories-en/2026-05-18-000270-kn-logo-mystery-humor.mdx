## 1. The Day of the Big Reveal

January 15, 2021, 5 a.m., Mobili headquarters' design center.

"It's the aesthetics of symmetry. It's perfect from any angle."

Park Ji-yeon, the senior designer of the team, answered confidently. That afternoon, the logo was released simultaneously in 14 languages worldwide.
