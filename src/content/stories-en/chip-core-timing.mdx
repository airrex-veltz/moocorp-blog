When Lee Su-yeon first saw the man sitting across the negotiation table, she instinctively knew he was an enemy.

Head of Supply Chain at Chungram Semiconductor. That's what his business card said. Jung Min-jun. The man from the company that had beaten Su-yeon in bids repeatedly for three years.

"You remember when EUV lithography equipment deliveries were delayed by more than half a year in 2023?" Min-jun pointed at the screen. The numbers were cold and precise. "The current situation is worse than that. Since both companies use the same packaging partner, we either collaborate or we both shut down our lines."

Su-yeon gritted her teeth. It was the right analysis. Much as she hated to admit it.

It was the fourth night. Only the two of them remained in the temporary office at the partner factory. The coffee had run out three hours ago, and a fluorescent light was flickering.

"Look at this part," Su-yeon said, pulling the screen toward her. "The packaging yield breaks here. I think it's the humidity variance in the process chamber."

Min-jun pulled his chair closer to the screen. His shoulder drew nearly level with hers. Su-yeon pretended not to notice.

"You're right," he said quietly. "How did you spot it? I couldn't find it in two days."

It was more pure curiosity than praise. In that moment, Su-yeon saw him for the first time not as a competitor, but as an engineer.

"Just luck," Su-yeon answered briefly.

Min-jun laughed. It was an expression she had never seen at the negotiation table.

At three in the morning, the yield data stabilized. The two of them reported their findings to their respective headquarters. The office lights went dark. As they walked toward the parking lot, Min-jun spoke first.

"If the same partner causes problems next time—"

Su-yeon stopped walking.

"If we're in a situation like this again," Min-jun continued, "I'll bring the coffee."

Su-yeon didn't answer. Instead, she started walking. Behind her, Min-jun's footsteps followed in the same direction.

The collaboration was over. Competition would resume again tomorrow morning. But tonight lingered quietly between them in that interval.
