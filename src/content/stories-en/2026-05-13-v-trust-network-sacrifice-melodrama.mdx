He was 47 years old. This job, taken late in life, might be his last chance.

That night, on a flight to Chicago, Dee wrote endlessly in a notebook. The passenger next to him asked, "What do you do?" Dee didn't answer. How could he explain? He was dreaming of the impossible. A world where competitors would join hands, share power, and win together.

Some listened. But the price was steep.
