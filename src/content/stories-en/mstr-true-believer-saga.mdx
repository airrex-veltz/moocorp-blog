At three in the morning, Park Jun-hyuk stood alone in the middle of an empty office.

Twelve screens cast a blue glow on his face. **HBIZ** stock had plummeted 38 percent in a single day. The board of Han-Mi Tech demanded an emergency session, and three institutional investors had already hung up on him. Lee Su-jin, the executive secretary, stood in the doorway and spoke.

"CEO, the coin price is falling again. It's below twenty thousand dollars."

Park Jun-hyuk did not turn his head.

"I know."

He recalled autumn 2021. On the day Bitcoin hit sixty-nine thousand dollars, the media had called him a genius. Now the same media called him a gambler. The numbers had changed, but the certainty had not. His decision to convert half of the company's retained earnings into Bitcoin had shocked the board and triggered shareholder protests.

Lee Su-jin spoke again. "Shouldn't we consider cutting our losses?"

Park Jun-hyuk looked at her for the first time. The shadows under his eyes spoke before his answer did.

"If we sell, we've already lost."

The numbers on the screens kept falling. He pulled the chair closer and sat. It was a long night. Whether it was the right decision would not be known until morning came.
