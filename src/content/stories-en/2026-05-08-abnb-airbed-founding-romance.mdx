"Joe, look at this." Brian pointed at the screen. "Hotels are fully booked. We have air mattresses at our place, right? What if we set up three of them and provide breakfast... wouldn't someone stay with us?"

Those words struck deep in Brian's heart. This was not merely a way to pay rent. This was trust. It was inviting a stranger into your home and believing in that person. And seeing that trust return.
