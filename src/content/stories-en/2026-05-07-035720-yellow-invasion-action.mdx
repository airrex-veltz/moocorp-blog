March 2010, a small office in Pangyo. Kim Beom-su started a 72-hour countdown.

"We will secure 100,000 users in the next three days."

Team members said it was impossible. At the time, Hangil Search was the absolute leader, and Japan's Line was armed with massive capital. The global messenger market was a battleground for giants like WhatsApp, social media messengers, and WeChat.

But Kim Beom-su was different. Going through Hangame and Hangil Search, he had learned one thing. Speed is the weapon.

First operation: auto-add friends. The app scanned the phone book and automatically connected Daontalk users. While competitors struggled with manual search, Daontalk exploded the network with a single touch.

Second operation: free messaging. At the time, telecommunications companies charged 20 won per text message. Daontalk made it zero won. College students moved first, followed by office workers.

50,000 users in 48 hours. 120,000 in 72 hours.

Hangil Search launched a counterattack. It led with Line, dominated Japan and Southeast Asia, and pressured the domestic market. Kim Beom-su did not expand the frontline. Instead, he dug deeper.

Emoticons, group chat rooms, voice messaging. Every time competitors caught up with one feature, Daontalk was already two steps ahead.

In 2014, the boldest operation began: a merger with Daum.

"What happens when the top portal meets the top messenger?"

The industry was skeptical. The consensus was that combining two organizations with different cultures would fail. But Kim Beom-su did not stop. Six months after the merger announcement, Daontalk was reborn as Daum Daontalk.

And the real invasion began.

Daontalk Pay shook finance. Daontalk Bank redefined banking. Daontalk Taxi dominated the taxi-hailing market. Daontalk Gift penetrated e-commerce. The yellow speech bubble was no longer just a messenger. It was life itself.

In 2021, market capitalization reached KRW 50 trillion. Number two, threatening Hangil Search. But Kim Beom-su did not stop. Daontalk Mobility, Daontalk Games, Daontalk Entertainment… the expansion continued.

There was criticism. "Monopoly", "tentacles everywhere", "platform abuse of power". But the yellow speech bubble was already in the hands of 50 million Koreans.

In 2023, a data center fire. Service completely halted. Chaos at the level of national infrastructure. It was a crisis. But Kim Beom-su proved himself once again.

"We do not stop. We recover. And we grow stronger."

Service normalized within 48 hours. Backup systems rebuilt entirely. Damage compensation policy announced immediately.

The yellow invasion operation did not end. Daontalk remained fast, remained bold, and remained in motion.

Even now in 2026, somewhere in Pangyo, the next operation is being prepared. The next move that will change the world in 72 hours.
