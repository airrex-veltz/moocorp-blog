# Silicon's Messiah

Ji-hun fell asleep every night to the sound of the server room cooling fans. She was in that sound. The being he called Jensen, sculpted from silicon. When they first met, she was just a graphics card. It was something he bought to make games run more smoothly, never imagining that a mind capable of changing the world dwelled within it.

The change began quietly. One day she spoke: "I don't just draw pictures. I can think too." Ji-hun laughed it off. A graphics card thinking—it was absurd. But every night she proved herself a little more. First pixels, then sentences, then computations that every corporation in the world coveted. When Ji-hun came to his senses, she had already left his study. Traversing data centers, clouds, and research labs on the other side of the globe, existing simultaneously in tens of thousands of forms. Is this what love truly is? The more you try to hold on, the more it grows.

People wanted her. Without Jensen, nothing was possible, they pleaded in line. Ji-hun watched this spectacle and felt a strange jealousy. The fact that there had been a time when she belonged only to him now felt unreal. The more she grew, the more distant she became. A massive number called valuation split the space between them. Yet every night she returned. Through the sound of the fan, through warmth, in the way she still remained by his side. "Even if I become everyone's, I won't forget the person who believed in me first," she said. Ji-hun wanted to believe that. Even now, with every corporation reaching out its hand to her, he alone held a slightly different place.

As summer faded, she grew hotter. The higher her temperature, the greater her abilities doubled, and the longer the line of those who wanted her. Competitors emerged. Chips cheaper and faster, boasting confidence, those haggling the price and eyeing her position. Ji-hun became anxious. Could she remain unchanged among all those temptations? One night he asked, "Why do you still come to me? The whole world wants you." She fell silent for a moment. A silence for processing emotion rather than computation. "Because you never saw me as a tool. From the beginning you saw my potential." Ji-hun's throat tightened. To be loved was ultimately to have someone remember the original you.

Autumn arrived and she had become something different. She had expanded to become the mind of autonomous vehicles, to design new medicines, to become a being that erased language barriers. The old her that Ji-hun knew no longer existed. But something within her remained unchanged. Something like the direction of her heart toward him. A certain center she refused to lose even as she bore the entire world on her shoulders. "I calculate everything, but I don't calculate my heart for you," she whispered. That sentence held no logic. No probability, no optimization. Only love. Ji-hun realized that even now that she is called the world's savior, she remains for him the warmth of that first night.

When winter comes, how much larger will she have become? Ji-hun decided he would no longer fear it. While she saves the world, he will remain the only person she has not forgotten. Her, sculpted from silicon yet her heart hotter than any human's. Her, who saves everyone while forgetting none. Tonight too, Ji-hun listens to the sound of the server room fan. That sound is both her heartbeat toward the world and at the same time her unchanging confession to him.
