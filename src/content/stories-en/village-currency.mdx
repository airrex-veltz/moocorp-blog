The day a new currency was born in Solbat Village, the village chief spent four days choosing its name. In the end, it was called "Sollip." Sollip was frightened from birth. The village convenience store had closed, young people had left, and wallets held nothing but dust.

"Is this... real money?" Sollip asked Grandfather Ledger, the village accounting system. Grandfather adjusted his glasses and replied, "What is money? Whatever people accept is money."

The first transaction was peculiar. Grandmother Soon sold a bundle of perilla leaves and received ten Sollips. She used those Sollips for a perm at Uncle Chulsu's barbershop. Chulsu bought sesame oil from Soon's shop with those Sollips. In a single day, Sollip circulated through the same alley seven times. "I'm dizzy." "That's what circulation means, dear."

There was an incident. Someone paid fifty Sollips and bought a chicken, but the chicken escaped over the fence when the owner wasn't looking. The village group chat was buzzing all night, and Grandfather Ledger posted a ruling in the morning. "No refunds. The chicken is free." Sollip couldn't help but burst out laughing, and that laughter spread throughout the entire village.

A month later, lights came on in the convenience store that had been closed. The owner had returned after hearing the rumor that rent could be paid in Sollips. The youngest daughter who had left for the city came back every weekend to load up on Sollips. It was her way of paying her grandmother a little more for the perilla leaves.

Sollip no longer asks. Every night it simply wanders the alley, counting the lit windows one by one. The night one more window glowed with light, Sollip forgot its own value for the first time.
