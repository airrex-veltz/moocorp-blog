"The accelerator cluster again."

Min-jun beside her set down his coffee cup with a murmur. He'd been repeating the same words for the past two months.

The legacy air-cooling system's limit was 15 kilowatts per rack. The numbers differed by more than seven times.

"We have to engage emergency shutdown."

Gae-yeon said. Min-jun shook his head.

"Then we incur breach penalties. Three clients are running real-time inference services."

Gae-yeon moved first.

Cheonghanytech was the company that had pioneered installing direct liquid cooling kits onto the back of racks, drawing heat out to external cooling towers. Normal lead time was three weeks. But it was the middle of the night.

She picked up the phone.

"Can you send a truck right now? G-7 is dying."

"We have four module sets. We'll be there within six hours."
