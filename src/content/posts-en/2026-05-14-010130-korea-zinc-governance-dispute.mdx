Korea Zinc faces intense market scrutiny amid a management rights dispute between MBK Partners and Youngpoong. As the nation's largest zinc refiner leverages its market dominance and pursues battery materials expansion, corporate governance uncertainty and a premium valuation converge in a compelling moment.

<KeyPoints>
- MBK-Youngpoong proxy fight in progress, attempting to secure 40% stake
- KRW 1 trillion+ share buyback and cancellation plan announced
- Global zinc price strength (LME zinc YTD +8%)
- Battery cathode material business expansion driving new growth
- Management uncertainty risk if governance dispute prolongs
</KeyPoints>

## Company Position

Korea Zinc ranks first in South Korea's zinc refining market with annual refining capacity exceeding 600,000 tons. Zinc refining accounts for 60% of revenue, copper, lead, and precious metals comprise 30%, and battery materials and other new businesses make up 10%. Recently, as MBK Partners pursues approximately 40% stake acquisition, the governance battle with Youngpoong intensified, driving average daily trading volume above KRW 30 billion and commanding market attention.

Market capitalization stands at approximately KRW 7 trillion, with valuation reflecting a management dispute premium. Over the past 30 days, shares exhibit high volatility and trading volume surges.

## Fundamentals

Korea Zinc's business structure comprises traditional zinc refining operations and battery materials as a new growth driver. The zinc refining business dominates the domestic market with annual production capacity exceeding 600,000 tons, while rising global zinc prices drive operational improvement.

LME zinc prices have risen 8% year-to-date, reflecting supply constraints. This is expected to positively impact Korea Zinc's refining margins.

In new business, the company is expanding its lithium-ion cathode material operations. The company targets raising battery materials revenue to 15% by 2025, marking a transition from a pure refiner to a materials company.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Management Dispute and Share Buyback">
In response to MBK Partners' stake acquisition attempt, the company announced a share buyback and cancellation plan totaling over KRW 1 trillion. While this may support the share price near-term, the long-term direction of corporate governance change remains uncertain.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Global Zinc Price Strength">
Global zinc prices continue to strengthen due to supply constraints. LME zinc has risen 8% year-to-date, expected to boost Korea Zinc's refining margins.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Prolonged governance dispute may sustain management uncertainty. Further, current valuations embed a significant dispute premium, creating valuation risk. Downside metal price risk merits consideration if global growth slows.
</Callout>

## Implications

Korea Zinc sits at the intersection of a governance dispute and improving operational fundamentals. Key watch points are corporate structure shifts, buyback scale, global zinc price trends, and visible battery materials business progress.

Near-term share volatility hinges on the dispute's direction and buyback execution. Medium to long-term, the battery materials business's growth and profitability improvement will be crucial to company valuation reassessment.
