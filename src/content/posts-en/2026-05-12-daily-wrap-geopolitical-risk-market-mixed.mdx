Iran's nuclear issues and Middle East tensions intensified, spreading unease across global financial markets. Investors shifted toward defensive assets, while tech stocks and cyclically sensitive stocks faced weakness.

## Today's Markets

U.S. stocks closed slightly lower. The S&P 500 fell 0.15% to $738.18, while the Nasdaq declined 0.85% to $707.24. The Russell 2000, a small-cap index, dropped 0.97% to $282.57.

Korea's market faced greater impact. The iShares MSCI Korea ETF plunged 7.44% to $178.50, demonstrating simultaneous pressure from geopolitical risk and the semiconductor sector.

Among major stocks, Nvidia rose 0.61% to $220.78, showcasing semiconductor leadership resilience. Apple also gained 0.72% to $294.80, but Tesla fell 2.60% to $433.45, while Microsoft declined 1.19% to $407.77.

## Sector Trends

Defensive sectors led the market. Healthcare surged 1.96% to the front, Consumer Staples gained 1.28%, and Financials rose 0.78%.

In contrast, Technology fell 1.51%, recording the largest decline. Consumer Discretionary dropped 0.90%, and Industrials fell 0.39%, with cyclical stocks broadly weakening. This is a typical risk-off environment.

## What to Watch Tomorrow

According to Reuters, Saudi Arabia launched covert attacks on Iran, while Iran warned it would enrich uranium to weapons grade if attacked. An earthquake was also reported in Tehran.

With geopolitical tensions unlikely to ease in the near term, volatility is expected to persist. The possibility of inflation resurgence due to energy price fluctuations remains a key point to monitor.
