EcoProbm is a global leader in high-nickel NCM cathode materials, ranked second globally. Following a loss-making year in 2024 amid falling nickel prices and intense Chinese competition, the company's profitability recovery hinges on North American EV demand revival and technological competitiveness.

- Operating loss of KRW 145 billion in 2024, with operating margin at -5.2%

- Recovery of North American EV demand and battery makers' capacity expansion restart as key catalysts
- Nickel price volatility and price competition from Chinese players pose significant risks
</KeyPoints>

## Company Status

EcoProbm, a subsidiary of the EcoProbe Group, specializes in high-nickel NCM cathode material production. Key customers include LG Energy Solution (40%) and Samsung SDI (25%), providing indirect exposure to the global electric vehicle market through these battery manufacturers. Current market capitalization stands at approximately KRW 10.5 trillion, with the stock price moving in a trading range over the past 30 days before rising 2.3% amid increased trading volume.

## Fundamentals

EcoProbm recorded an operating loss of KRW 145 billion in 2024, marking a shift to losses. Revenue is expected to reach KRW 2.8 trillion in 2025, representing 15% growth year-over-year, but profitability improvement remains a critical variable. First-quarter 2025 revenue declined 8% year-over-year to KRW 68 billion, with falling nickel prices and aggressive pricing from Chinese competitors pressuring margins.

The company maintains 12% global market share in high-nickel NCM cathode materials, holding the second position behind China's CATL. Upon completion of capacity expansion at the Pohang facility in 2026, annual production capacity is expected to increase 50%, from 120,000 tons to 180,000 tons.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="North American EV Demand Recovery">
Major North American automakers including GM and Ford plan to expand high-nickel battery-equipped models starting in the second half of 2026. As utilization rates at LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI's North American plants increase, EcoProbm's order volumes are expected to grow accordingly.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Technological Competitiveness">
The company maintains technological advantages over Chinese competitors such as CNGR and GEM in the high-nickel segment, with mass production capabilities for products containing over 90% nickel as a key differentiator. South Korean government support for domestic sourcing of secondary battery materials also presents a positive factor.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Nickel prices currently stand stable at around $17,000 per ton on the LME, but further declines could increase cost pressures. Additionally, intensifying price competition from Chinese players may delay profitability recovery.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Price competition between Tesla and BYD has increased uncertainty in the electric vehicle market, and EcoProbm faces affiliate transaction risk due to high intra-group dealings. Major capital expenditures from Pohang facility expansion also present cash flow pressures.
</Callout>

## Implications

For EcoProbm to escape losses, concurrent recovery in North American EV demand and resumption of capacity expansion by the three battery makers are necessary. While high-nickel cathode material technological competitiveness remains valid, the rapid catch-up by Chinese competitors cannot be discounted, with profitability recovery potentially delayed to late 2026 or beyond. Attention should be paid to nickel price trends and order flow from LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI.
