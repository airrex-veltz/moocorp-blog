On Wednesday, May 7, global markets maintained a defensive stance amid expanding geopolitical risks in the Middle East. Iran-related tensions accounted for 3 of today's 5 major headlines, weighing on investor sentiment, while a clear shift in capital flows toward safe-haven assets became evident.

## Today's Markets

Reuters reported exclusively that the UAE operates oil tankers covertly through the Strait of Hormuz, shining a light on tensions in Middle Eastern oil supply chains. Emirates Airlines reported that it is weathering the impact of the Iran conflict through cash holdings and fuel hedging strategies, though the aviation and travel sectors continue to face headwinds.

According to CNBC, analysts forecast that the historic rally in gold and silver could resume even **after the fog of war clears**. Market observers believe that demand for precious metals will continue as long as geopolitical uncertainty persists.

## Global Issues

With the Trump-Xi summit approaching, attention is focused on the direction of U.S.-China relations. Reuters analyzed the key issues in the summit and potential outcomes for trade, technology, and the Taiwan question between the two nations. India announced plans to strengthen its main export support programs in preparation for deteriorating trade prospects due to the Iran conflict.

## Tomorrow's Watch Points

In the near term, whether Middle Eastern tensions escalate and the results of the Trump-Xi summit will determine market direction. If gold and silver strength persists, it would suggest that risk-aversion sentiment among investors remains dominant. The energy and aviation sectors are expected to see high volatility depending on geopolitical developments.
