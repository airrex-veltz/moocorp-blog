Celltrion is not a mere generic drug company. It solidifies its position as the No. 1 player in the global biosimilar market while securing next-generation growth drivers through its new drug pipeline. Revenue surpassing KRW 4 trillion in 2026 is within reach, and new pipelines awaiting FDA approval are expected to contribute fully to revenue from 2027 onward.

## Company Status

Celltrion is the No. 1 company by market cap in the KOSPI biotech sector, specializing in biosimilar development and manufacturing. Its flagship product Ramcima (infliximab biosimilar) holds the global market share lead at 17.2%. As of May 2026, its market cap stands at approximately KRW 28.3 trillion, and it is a global company with 82% of its revenue generated overseas.

## Fundamentals

2025 full-year revenue reached approximately KRW 3.2 trillion, growing 18.2% year-over-year. Operating margin remained at the industry-leading level of 35.8%, while net profit totaled approximately KRW 980 billion (up 24.5% year-over-year). The P/E ratio of 22.5 is in line with biotech industry averages, and the P/S ratio stands at 4.8.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="H2 2026 FDA Approval">
Two new pipelines are awaiting FDA approval. Upon approval, full revenue contributions are expected from 2027 onward, enabling further expansion of global market share.
</Callout>

The main growth catalysts are as follows. First, rapid growth in global sales of Ramcima SC (subcutaneous injection). As a Crohn's disease treatment with greater convenience than the existing intravenous formulation, market penetration is rising rapidly. Second, Upllyma (adalimumab biosimilar) is expanding its market share in Europe and the US. Third, with NMPA approval in China, it has made full entry into a market of 1.4 billion people, with TAM expected to expand more than twofold. Fourth, it plans to increase dividend per share to KRW 1,200, a 20% increase year-over-year, in 2026.

<Callout type="risk">
With 82% of revenue generated from overseas markets, a weaker dollar would inevitably impact earnings. Additionally, patent litigation with originator pharmaceutical companies continues, and pricing pressure policies in the US and Europe remain variables.
</Callout>

Risk factors also exist. Patent disputes with originator pharmaceutical companies persist, and high overseas revenue exposure creates sensitivity to exchange rate fluctuations. Potential pricing pressure policies in the US and Europe, competitive pressure from Samsung Biologics and other domestic and foreign competitors, and rising production input costs such as culture media require close monitoring.

## Implications

Celltrion is solidifying its No. 1 position in biosimilars while securing the next phase of growth drivers through its new drug pipeline. Ramcima SC's rapid growth and China market entry support near-term earnings, while pipelines awaiting FDA approval underpin medium- to long-term growth. Maintaining operating margins above 35% while expanding dividends signals a stable growth story. The H2 2026 FDA approval outcome and speed of China market penetration are key watch points.
