## Takeaway

The revenue growth is impressive, but the stock price reflects market skepticism about profitability. If management can outline a clear path to monetization in the May 21 earnings call, it could be a catalyst for a stock rally. Conversely, guidance for continued losses could trigger further declines.

The current stock price reflects low expectations. Trading near the 52-week low suggests that bad news has already been substantially priced in. This should be approached as a high-risk, high-volatility position, with sharp price swings around the earnings date in mind.
