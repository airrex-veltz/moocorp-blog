## Company Overview

Naver is Korea's leading search portal and a comprehensive internet platform spanning e-commerce, fintech, and cloud services. With a market capitalization of approximately KRW 34 trillion, it operates search advertising, SmartStore, Naver Pay, and LINE Yahoo, among others. Over the past 30 days, the stock rose 5% on positive sentiment from AI search feature updates.

## Fundamentals

Q1 2026 revenue grew 12% year-over-year, maintaining steady momentum. E-commerce segment GMV growth was the primary growth driver, while advertising revenue also recovered, maintaining an operating margin of around 15%.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="AI Search Commercialization">
Generative AI search powered by HyperCLOVA X will begin full-scale commercialization from Q2 2026. Differentiated search quality is expected to increase user engagement and advertising revenue.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Cloud Profitability Shift">
Naver Cloud is expected to achieve profitability in Q2 2026. Rising AI infrastructure demand and efficiency improvements will drive profitability gains.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Competition in e-commerce with Kakao and Coupang is intensifying, and the possibility of stricter platform regulation poses a risk. Potential changes to LINE Yahoo's ownership structure also increase uncertainty for the Japan business.
</Callout>

## Implications

Naver is building a medium to long-term growth story through proactive AI technology investment and e-commerce ecosystem expansion. Q2 earnings, user response to AI search, and cloud profitability shift are key monitoring points. We recommend positioning after confirming earnings momentum.
