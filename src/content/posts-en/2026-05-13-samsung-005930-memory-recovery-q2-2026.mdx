## Fundamentals

Memory division revenue in Q1 2026 is estimated to have grown approximately 15% year-over-year. Supported by improving semiconductor market conditions, Q2 operating margin is forecast to exceed 10%, and a P/E ratio of 18x is favorable relative to industry average.

Memory prices have begun to rebound after hitting bottom in Q1, and are showing upside momentum in Q2. DRAM and NAND prices are expected to rise 5-10% respectively, driven by completed inventory adjustments and increased AI server demand.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="AI Semiconductor Demand">
HBM3E supply expansion to drive increased shipments to major customers including Nvidia and AMD. Rising share of high-margin memory driving profitability gains.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Foundry Order Growth">
Expanded orders for next-generation GPUs from Nvidia and AMD. Foundry division securing new growth driver in revenue expansion.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Risk of smartphone and consumer electronics demand erosion from slowing Chinese economy. Won strength could weaken export competitiveness. Intensifying HBM market competition from SK Hynix and Micron.
</Callout>

## Implications

The memory chip market is transitioning into recovery as it passes through the trough. Expanding AI server demand and growing HBM3E supply are forecast to be key drivers of earnings improvement. On near-term weakness, the stock warrants investor attention from a medium- to long-term perspective.
