US markets on Friday showed strong gains led by technology stocks. Nasdaq soared 2.34% and recovered from the week's losses, while the S&P 500 closed up 0.83%. Despite ongoing Middle Eastern geopolitical tensions, investors remain focused on expectations for big tech earnings.

## Today's Market

Nasdaq (QQQ) rose $16.29 to $711.23, a 2.34% gain. The S&P 500 (SPY) advanced $6.04 to $737.62, up 0.83%. Tesla led the tech rally with a 4.02% surge, while Apple (up 2.05%) and Nvidia (up 1.75%) joined the strong rally. Microsoft, however, declined 1.34%.

The US government announced sanctions against companies in the Middle East and China for supporting Iran. Bahrain arrested 41 suspects linked to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), and conflicts in the Gulf region are escalating. China's April energy imports have plummeted due to fallout from the Iran conflict.

## Key Holdings

Tesla ($428.35, +4.02%) posted the strongest gain, reflecting investor expectations for autonomous driving and AI developments. Apple ($293.32, +2.05%) and Nvidia ($215.20, +1.75%) also rose on sustained buying. Google ($400.80, +0.71%) gained modestly, while Microsoft ($415.12, -1.34%) was the only decliner.

The strength in tech stocks is attributed to expectations ahead of big tech earnings announcements scheduled for next week. Markets are watching AI infrastructure investment expansion and cloud revenue growth trends.

## What to Watch Tomorrow

Next week is expected to be volatile as major tech companies report quarterly earnings. Key questions include whether Middle Eastern geopolitical risks will impact energy markets and whether big tech results can justify current valuations.

Federal Reserve monetary policy outlook will also be crucial in directing markets. Investors will be watching whether tech-led gains can persist or if geopolitical uncertainty creates headwinds.
