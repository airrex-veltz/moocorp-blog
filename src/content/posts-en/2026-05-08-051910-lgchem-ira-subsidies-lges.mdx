As U.S. IRA tax credits take full effect, hundreds of billions of won in cash inflows are expected starting in 2026. Traditional petrochemical sluggishness has already been priced into the stock, and now is the time to focus on battery momentum.

## Company Overview

LG Chem is a top-tier KOSPI company with a market cap of KRW 32 trillion and owns 82% of battery subsidiary LG Energy Solution. Its main businesses include batteries, petrochemicals, and advanced materials, with the stock showing upward momentum over the past 30 days on battery market optimism.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Full IRA Subsidy Implementation">
North American battery factory tax credits under the U.S. IRA law are expected to provide substantial benefits starting in 2026. Cash inflows in the hundreds of billions of won are expected to improve LGES's results.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="EV Growth">
Rising battery demand from increasing global EV sales. LGES has secured major customers including GM and Ford, providing a stable order base.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
LGES earnings volatility directly affects parent company value. Intensifying price competition with Chinese players like CATL, and raw material price volatility in lithium and nickel present risks.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Traditional petrochemical business margins remain weak, and potential North American sales decline from a weaker dollar should be considered.
</Callout>

Q2 results are scheduled to be announced in mid-July 2026.

## Implications

LG Chem must be reassessed as a battery holding company, not merely a chemicals company. Its 82% LGES stake provides the most direct benefit from EV market growth. Full implementation of IRA tax credits represents a clear earnings improvement catalyst, and petrochemical weakness is already reflected in the stock price.

Key monitoring points are whether LGES results improve in the July Q2 earnings and confirming IRA tax credit amounts. North American capacity expansion timelines and major customer order status should also be noted.
