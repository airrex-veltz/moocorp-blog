## Fundamentals

Tesla's P/E of 386.9x is 10.5 times Apple's 36.7x, reflecting the market's extreme growth expectations. However, this also means paying 386x earnings per dollar of profit, a level that carries significant downside risk in case of valuation compression. The P/B ratio of 18.2x is also elevated, indicating stock price significantly decoupled from underlying asset value.

Among 61 analyst coverers, 40% maintain a Hold opinion, with Sell ratings accounting for 13%. Opinions span the full spectrum from Strong Buy to Strong Sell, indicating peak market uncertainty.

## Implications

Tesla is a high-risk, high-reward stock trading at 78% higher volatility than the market average (Beta 1.78). While the 44% recovery from the 52-week low is positive, it is worth monitoring whether the stock can sustain additional upward momentum as it remains 22% below its high.
