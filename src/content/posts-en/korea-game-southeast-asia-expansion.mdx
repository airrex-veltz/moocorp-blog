## Southeast Asia Game Market Growth Divides Korean Publisher Performance

Since 2026, two Korean game publisher giants show diverging results. **NC** (036570) maintains strong momentum: 101.5% revenue growth, 29.5% net margin. **Netmarble** (251270) lags at 4.4% revenue growth, 13.7% net margin. Behind this gap lies Southeast Asia's rapidly maturing mobile game market.

## Southeast Asia: Smartphone Proliferation and Expanding Ad/Subscription Revenue Models

Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam and other Southeast Asian nations accelerate smartphone penetration. As smartphone adoption reaches critical mass, new user cohorts accessing mobile games expand rapidly. Simultaneously, ad-based and subscription-based revenue models are taking root locally. The region is transitioning from pure in-app purchase monetization toward coexisting diversified revenue streams.

## NC's 101.5% Growth vs. Netmarble's 4.4% Stagnation: What's Different?

By 2025 fiscal year, NC reported KRW 1.5 trillion revenue with KRW 16.1 billion operating income (as of Oct 12, 2026). Netmarble's 2025 net income was reported at KRW 230.8 billion. NC's 101.5% revenue growth (Oct 12, 2026) versus Netmarble's 4.4% (Oct 12, 2026) difference signals divergent responsiveness to Southeast Asia market dynamics.

## Four Factors Driving NC's Strength

**1. Southeast Asia Ad Revenue Expansion**: Rising smartphone users drive increased ad exposure in games. NC has rapidly adapted ad-based monetization models to local demand.

**2. Subscription Model Localization**: Monthly subscription games gain acceptance in Philippines and Indonesia. NC's portfolio aligns well with this appetite.

**3. FX Advantage**: Local currency weakness in Southeast Asia works in NC's favor on dollar-basis revenue conversion, especially for PC-mobile hybrid revenue streams.

**4. Game Portfolio Diversity**: NC launches new titles capturing new users, while Netmarble relies heavily on legacy franchises. NC's agility against fast-shifting Southeast Asian preferences emerges in the growth differential.

## NC's Structural Edge, Netmarble's Relative Weakness

**High net margins signal operating efficiency**: NC's 29.5% net margin (Oct 12, 2026) reflects profitable growth, not just revenue scaling. Efficient cost management and localization strategy in Southeast Asia yielded results.

**Netmarble's Underperformance**: 13.7% net margin (Oct 12, 2026) and 4.4% revenue growth signal existing revenue source maturation. Southeast Asia response lagged, likely.

## Summary

Southeast Asia's mobile game market smartphone expansion and ad/subscription model spread are polarizing Korean game publisher performance. Firms that quickly adapted to new markets and revenue models show strength; those relying on legacy portfolios show weakness. The performance differential translates directly to revenue and profitability.
