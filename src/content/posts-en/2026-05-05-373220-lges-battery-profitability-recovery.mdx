## Fundamentals

LG Energy Solution's Q1 2026 results are likely to show a return to profitability. Following the break-even point in Q4 2025, falling lithium prices and increased US factory utilization have aligned to support this trend. The company has provided 2026 fiscal year operating profit guidance of KRW 1.5–2.0 trillion, with a target of expanding operating margin to 8–10% from 2027 onward.

The core of cost improvement is falling lithium prices. Lithium prices fell 30–40% in 2024–2025, significantly reducing the raw material cost burden—the largest component in battery production. In addition, Ultium Cells, the joint venture with GM, has begun full-scale operations at its Ohio and Tennessee factories, increasing the company's North American production share. Under the IRA (Inflation Reduction Act), batteries produced locally in the US receive per-kWh incentives, which contribute directly to net profit.

## Catalysts and Risks

<Callout type="catalyst" title="North American Market Expansion and IRA">
Full-scale operations of GM Ultium Cells joint venture factory increasing local US production share. IRA incentives contribute directly to profitability, and expanded supply of Tesla 4680 cylindrical batteries is planned.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Increased ESS Demand">
Power grid stabilization and renewable energy expansion driving sharp growth in ESS demand. A new growth engine for battery makers.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
Cost competitiveness gap relative to CATL. Low market share in China, concerns about slower EV adoption in North America and Europe. Capex spending plan of approximately KRW 8 trillion through 2026 creates cash burn pressure.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
High dependency on GM at approximately 25%. Risk of stranded assets in existing production lines in the event of transition to next-generation technologies such as solid-state batteries.
</Callout>

## Takeaways

LG Energy Solution has entered a phase of earnings recovery through cost improvement and US factory operations. With a 15% global market share ranking second after CATL, the company benefits from IRA support and structural tailwinds of growing ESS demand. However, the cost competitiveness gap relative to CATL, massive capex burden, and concentrated GM dependency remain significant risk factors.

Q1 results are scheduled to be released mid-May, with the return to profitability and potential revision of full-year guidance being key points to watch. A P/E multiple of 25–30x reflects a growth premium; the pace of future margin improvement and capex recovery timeline will be the basis for valuation reassessment.
