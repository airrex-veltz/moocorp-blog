## Battery Material Oversupply Cuts Core EV Manufacturing Costs by 30%

Lithium mining expansion in Australia and Chile has been completed, and global battery raw material supply now exceeds demand. This is not merely a price decline. It is a turning point where EV manufacturers are structurally reassessing battery pack costs.

## Mining Expansion and Supply Status

Lithium mines in Australia's Pilbara and Chile's Atacama regions completed expansion during 2024–2025. Simultaneously, cobalt and nickel mining have also expanded. Supply growth now outpaces EV demand growth. As a result, spot market prices for lithium and cobalt have fallen sharply from their 2023 peaks.

## Why Battery Costs Are Collapsing Now: Four Factors

**1. Supply Leadership**: Mining consolidation and efficiency improvements have significantly reduced extraction costs. Australian miners' automation and economies of scale are now 20–30% cheaper than Chile's legacy mines. Suppliers are competing for market share through price cuts.

**2. Raw Materials' Weight in Battery Pack Manufacturing**: Raw materials represent a significant share of battery pack manufacturing costs. When raw material costs fall sharply, battery pack prices fall accordingly. This translates to reduced total manufacturing costs for finished EVs.

**3. Contract Duration**: Most existing mining purchase contracts for battery makers are structured for 2–3 year terms. New lower-cost supply is reflected with each contract renewal, accelerating cost declines.

**4. Demand Slowdown Reverses Negotiating Power**: EV sales growth has decelerated, strengthening battery makers' position as buyers. Large battery manufacturers like **BYD** and **CATL** can now demand price reductions from mining firms. Supply oversupply has multiplied their bargaining power.

## Structural Shifts in Battery Manufacturing and the EV Industry

**Supply Chain Risk Reversal**: While supply shortages were the bottleneck for EV manufacturing over the past two years, meeting demand is now harder. This shifts investment priorities for miners and battery makers toward cost efficiency.

**EV Price Competition**: Battery cost reductions take 6–12 months to translate into finished car prices. Auto suppliers like **LG** and car makers now have room to lower consumer prices. This can reignite EV demand.

## Takeaway

Mining expansion completion has fundamentally lowered battery pack costs. This is a defining moment for the EV industry's cost structure—a first-generation recalibration. The consumer price competition era has already begun.
