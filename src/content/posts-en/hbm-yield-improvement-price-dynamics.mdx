Do not miss the signal sent by HBM yield curves. As 3rd generation HBM production yields improve sharply, the global memory semiconductor landscape stands at a historic inflection point.

---

## Why Yield Improvement Signals Price Decline

High-bandwidth memory is the AI accelerator bottleneck. To overcome bandwidth limits of GPUs like NVIDIA H100 and H200, memory-side innovation is essential. 3rd generation HBM (HBM3e) started with low yields in Q1 2024. That has now improved substantially—the market interprets this as confirmation of price competition months ahead.

As yield rises, cost falls. With cost falling, price cannot remain unchanged. Competitors have begun capacity expansion.

## Competitor Expansion Timing and Oversupply Scenario

**SK Hynix** and Samsung have simultaneously begun expanding HBM mass production. SK Hynix announced expansion at its Icheon facility to tens of millions of units per quarter. Samsung is preparing to restart lines in Pyeongtak and Hwaseong.

If they begin full shipments in Q4 2025 through Q1 2026, the market faces oversupply. While AI accelerator demand follows trend extrapolation, HBM supply is not linear but step-function. Once an expansion line begins operation, supply can double within a year.

This changes the price scenario.

## Four Pressure Factors from Late 2025 to Early 2026

**1. Sharp Rise in Yield Curves**:
As 3rd generation HBM yields improve substantially, production costs plummet. The early premium price structure (over $1,000 per unit) becomes unsustainable.

**2. Simultaneous Competitor Expansion**:
SK Hynix and Samsung expand capacity in roughly the same timeframe. Memory chip demand forecasting is imperfect. The moment supply catches demand, price decline accelerates sharply.

**3. Forward Contract Price Signals**:
The memory industry standard is 3-6 month forward contracts. Buyers (large cloud providers, AI chip designers) may already be quoting lower price levels.

**4. Profit Margin Pressure**:
**Micron** (MU ticker) closed October 13, 2026 at $953.19, near the upper end of its 6-month range of $424.79-$1,254.81. A profit margin of 63.8% reflects current premium pricing. Oversupply could cause this margin to collapse.

## Structural Strengths and Weaknesses in Margin Restructuring

**Strength: Micron's Technology Lead**

Micron's annual revenue growth is 379.3% (October 13, 2026 basis). This shows that HBM demand surge has favored the U.S. camp. If technology leadership remains unshaken, market share defense is possible even amid price decline.

**Weakness: Korean Camp Losses**

SK Hynix (SK ticker) has recorded declines of -22.97% in one month and -36.61% in three months (October 13, 2026 basis). If expansion timing precedes price decline, new capacity sells at lower prices, causing severe losses. Worse if shipment timing lags one to two quarters.

**Remaining Uncertainty: Demand Risk**

AI infrastructure investment could slow more than expected. If so, oversupply worsens. Prices fall faster and deeper.

## Summary

The signal that 3rd generation HBM yields reached the 90% range is not merely technical progress. It's a warning that supply structure will fundamentally change from late 2025 onward. Price decline and margin restructuring are predetermined, not accidental.
