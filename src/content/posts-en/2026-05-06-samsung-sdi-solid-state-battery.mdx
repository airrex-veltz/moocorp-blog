# Samsung SDI Reshapes the EV Battery Market with Solid-State Technology

## A Leader in Global EV Battery Markets

Samsung SDI (006400) is a **global Top 5 electric vehicle battery manufacturer**, maintaining long-term supply contracts with major automakers including BMW, Ford, and Hyundai. The most notable catalyst in 2026 is **the start of solid-state battery pilot production**. This marks Samsung SDI's entry into the leading group in next-generation battery technology competition.

---

## Company Overview

Samsung SDI is a global battery specialist producing electric vehicle (EV) batteries, energy storage systems (ESS), and small-format batteries. The company plans to expand EV battery production capacity to **over 200 GWh by 2027**, roughly double the current level.

The ESS division stands out with strong growth, recording **40% year-over-year revenue increase**. Growing renewable energy adoption and rising demand for grid stabilization are driving ESS market expansion, positioning Samsung SDI as a key supplier in this sector.

---

## Core Growth Drivers

### 1. Solid-State Battery Pilot Production Launch (2026)

**Solid-state batteries** deliver **higher energy density, faster charging, and superior safety** compared to conventional lithium-ion batteries. Samsung SDI is deploying solid-state battery pilot lines starting in 2026, positioning itself ahead in next-generation technology competition.

**Key Differentiators**:
- **Fast Charging**: 80% charge in under 10 minutes
- **High Energy Density**: 50%+ greater driving range at the same volume
- **Safety**: Solid electrolyte instead of liquid electrolyte minimizes fire risk

When commercialized, this technology addresses electric vehicles' greatest weaknesses—**charging time** and **driving range**—significantly strengthening Samsung SDI's market position.

### 2. Production Capacity Expansion to Over 200 GWh (2027 Target)

The global electric vehicle market is expected to grow at **over 20% annually through 2027**. To meet this, Samsung SDI is building new production lines in the United States, Europe, and Asia, planning to expand capacity to **over 200 GWh by 2027**.

This scale enables battery supply for approximately **4 million electric vehicles**, positioning the company to meet rising demand from major OEM customers including BMW, Ford, and Hyundai.

### 3. Strategic OEM Partnerships

Samsung SDI maintains long-term supply contracts with global automakers:
- **BMW**: Exclusive battery supply for next-generation electric vehicle platforms
- **Ford**: Battery supply for North American electric trucks and SUVs
- **Hyundai**: Battery supply for major EV models across the Hyundai Motor Group

These partnerships establish **stable demand foundations** and offer **quality and reliability advantages** over Chinese competitors.

### 4. ESS Market Growth (+40% Year-over-Year)

Energy storage system (ESS) revenue grew **40% year-over-year**, emerging as a new growth pillar. Rapid ESS market expansion is driven by accelerating renewable energy adoption and mounting grid stabilization demands.

Samsung SDI is supplying large-scale ESS solutions and expanding partnerships with **power companies in the United States and Europe**.
