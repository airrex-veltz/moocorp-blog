**Moo Corp** is a research project that screens approximately 8,500 listed companies in the US and Korea weekly and publishes analyses daily in different formats depending on timing.

<KeyPoints>
- US + Korea all listed companies (~8,500)
- 5 automated routines, ~36 analyses published weekly
- 3 types of content: stock deep-dive, market summary, sector spotlight
- All analyses are for information purposes and are not investment recommendations
</KeyPoints>

## Publishing Schedule

Routines automatically launch daily according to market hours.

<DataTable caption="Publishing routines">
| Routine | Time (KST) | Format | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| US Morning Brief | Daily 09:00 | Stock analysis | 2 reports |
| Weekly Sector Spotlight | Weekdays 12:00 | Sector deep-dive | 1 report |
| KR Afternoon Brief | Weekdays 16:30 | Stock analysis | 2 reports |
| Daily Market Wrap | Daily 22:00 | Market summary | 1 report |
| Sunday Market Screening | Sunday 09:00 | Backlog update | (internal) |
</DataTable>

## Content Types

### Stock Deep-Dive

Deep analysis of one US or Korean listed company. Covers fundamentals, catalysts, and risks. Displays price charts, key metric cards, and analyst opinions visually.

### Market Summary (Daily Wrap)

Published daily at 22:00 KST. All key indices in the US and Korea, sector performance, notable stocks, and 5 headlines at a glance. Light-toned daily summary.

### Sector Spotlight (Daily Themes)

| Day | Theme |
|---|---|
| Monday | AI / Cloud |

| Wednesday | Biotech / Healthcare |

| Friday | Clean Energy / Mobility |
