This article showcases visual components provided by Moo Corp blog. All analysis articles follow monochrome design, and emphasis or visualization is handled through Material Symbols icons and MDX components.

## Key Summary Box

<KeyPoints>
- Reduced visual noise with a monochrome color palette
- Indicate meaning through Material Symbols icons
- Embed charts, statistics, and callouts in the body through MDX components
- Ad slots are automatically placed at the top and bottom of the body
</KeyPoints>

## Statistics Cards (StatGrid + StatCard)

Shows key company metrics at a glance in the first section of a stock analysis article.

<StatGrid cols={3}>
  <StatCard label="Market Cap" value="$4.85T" trend="up" context="Down 4.6% vs. previous day" />
  <StatCard label="ROE" value="104%" context="Top in industry" />
  <StatCard label="Net Margin" value="55.6%" trend="up" />
</StatGrid>

## Callout

Separates and emphasizes risks or catalysts from the body flow.

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Upcoming Catalyst">
Q2 earnings announcement on May 20. Market consensus: EPS $1.79, revenue $80B.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
ROE of 104% includes some inflation from buybacks. Should be viewed separately from actual profitability.
</Callout>

## Quote

Separates key sentences from the body to provide visual rest.

<Quote cite="Jensen Huang, NVIDIA CEO">
AI infrastructure investment is just beginning. Over the next four years, approximately 1 trillion dollars in data center upgrades will take place.
</Quote>

## Conclusion

This design system prioritizes analysis readability. Rather than using color or emoji for emphasis, it establishes information hierarchy through typography and structural components.
