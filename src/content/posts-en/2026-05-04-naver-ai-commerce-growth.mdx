## Company Overview

Naver is a comprehensive internet platform spanning search, commerce, fintech, content, and cloud. With a market cap of KRW 30–35 trillion, it ranks No. 1 in Korea's IT sector; its monthly active users of 45 million represent over 90% of Korea's internet population. Over the past 30 days, the stock has fluctuated in the KRW 180,000–200,000 band, reflecting crossed currents of AI search transition uncertainty and commerce growth optimism.

## Fundamentals

For fiscal year 2025, Naver recorded KRW 11 trillion in revenue and KRW 1.5–1.8 trillion in operating profit, representing 12–15% year-over-year growth. Operating margin remains at 13–16% thanks to margin improvement in ads and commerce, with net income around KRW 1.2 trillion.

Growth drivers break down into four areas. First, AI search transition: Naver has commercialized its proprietary LLM, HyperCLOVA X, launched AI chatbot Q in Q1 2026, and is experimenting with generative AI search ad models. Second, commerce platform expansion: Smart Store annual transaction volume surpassed KRW 40 trillion, Naver Pay transaction volume exceeded KRW 80 trillion with 25% year-over-year growth. Live commerce strengthening is intensifying competition with Coupang.

Third, global webtoon expansion: Naver Webtoon is pursuing a New York Stock Exchange IPO in H2 2026, with enterprise value estimated at KRW 3–4 trillion. It has achieved success in global expansion with over 10 million monthly active users in North America and Europe. Fourth, cloud growth: Cloud revenue grew 40–50% year-over-year on rising AI infrastructure demand, with public cloud contract wins also expanding.

## Catalysts and Risks

Three catalysts to watch over the next 3–6 months:

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Q1 Earnings Release">
Expected in early May 2026. Consensus operating profit of KRW 400–450 billion, implying 10–15% growth from the prior quarter. Key question: recovery in search ad revenue.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="HyperCLOVA X Monetization">
B2B AI service monetization planned for Q2 2026. Initial target: 1,000 enterprise customers with KRW 50 billion annual revenue.
</Callout>

<Callout type="catalyst" title="Naver Webtoon IPO">
NYSE listing pursued in H2 2026. Estimated IPO size of KRW 500–700 billion. Success would unlock value creation for Naver's 50% stake.
</Callout>

Medium-term, LINE Yahoo restructuring deserves attention. Potential for 100% LINE stake acquisition should Japanese regulatory constraints ease, enabling full market control in Japan. Additionally, the goal is to establish a generative AI search ad model by 2027, raising search ad revenue by 20%.

Four major risks:

<Callout type="risk">
**Competitive risk**: Intensified global competition with Google and Bing AI search (ChatGPT Search, Gemini). Commerce competition with Coupang Rocket. Messenger and fintech competition with Kakao.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
**Regulatory risk**: LINE Yahoo Japan foreign ownership rules constrain stake structure. Korean platform regulations (app store commissions, search algorithm fairness). LINE data breach aftermath.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
**Technology risk**: AI transition failure if HyperCLOVA X underperforms GPT and Gemini. Declining traditional search traffic from generative AI. Potential ad CPM erosion.
</Callout>

<Callout type="risk">
**Valuation risk**: P/E compression if growth slows while elevated P/E (25–30x) persists. Webtoon IPO failure or delay. LINE stake complexity risking undervaluation.
</Callout>

## Implications

Naver seeks a turnaround amid AI search transition by simultaneously rolling out proprietary LLM commercialization and commerce expansion. Q1 earnings, HyperCLOVA X monetization results, and Webtoon IPO progress will determine near-term share price direction. The company's commanding 58% domestic search share and KRW 40 trillion Smart Store volume provide a solid foundation, but competition from Google and Coupang, alongside LINE Yahoo Japan regulatory risks, remain key watch points.
