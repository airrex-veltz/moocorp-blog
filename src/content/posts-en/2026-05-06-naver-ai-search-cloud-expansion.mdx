# Naver: Advancing with AI Search and Cloud

## Korea's Platform's AI Transformation

NAVER (035420) is evolving beyond a simple search portal into an **AI technology platform**. Building on **over 70% market share** in Korea's search market, it is constructing new growth engines through generative AI integration and cloud business expansion.

---

## Company Overview

NAVER is Korea's largest internet platform company, with a diversified business portfolio spanning search, commerce, webtoons, and cloud. As of 2026, its e-commerce GMV (gross merchandise value) has **surpassed KRW 50 trillion**, and its cloud business is growing **over 30% year-over-year**.

Notably, **AI development investment increased 50% in 2026**, demonstrating NAVER's strategic commitment to position itself as an AI technology leader rather than merely a search company.

---

## Key Growth Drivers

### 1. Generative AI Search Enhancement

NAVER is revolutionizing user experience by integrating its proprietary large language model **HyperCLOVA X** into search. It is evolving from traditional keyword-based search to **conversational AI-powered search**, competing with Google's Bard and Microsoft's Bing AI.

**The competitive advantage** is **Korean language specialization**. HyperCLOVA X delivers world-class performance in Korean natural language processing, securing a competitive edge over global tech giants in the local market.

### 2. Large-Scale Cloud Business Expansion

NAVER Cloud has emerged as the **second growth pillar**, achieving **over 30% year-over-year growth** in 2026. As Korean enterprises accelerate cloud migration, NAVER Cloud is strengthening its position as a local alternative to AWS, Azure, and GCP.

**AI workload optimization** and **regulatory compliance capabilities** (data sovereignty, personal information protection) serve as differentiators and contribute to capturing public sector and enterprise customers.

### 3. E-Commerce GMV Surpassing KRW 50 Trillion

NAVER Shopping's gross merchandise value has **surpassed KRW 50 trillion**, establishing itself as Korea's largest e-commerce platform. Personalized recommendation algorithms powered by generative AI are improving conversion rates, while live commerce and SmartStore ecosystem are driving merchant expansion.

### 4. Global Webtoon IP Expansion

NAVER's webtoon business is evolving beyond content provision into a **global IP business**. It is strengthening its webtoon platform presence in North America and Southeast Asia, while diversifying revenue through IP licensing partnerships with OTT services like Netflix.

## Investment Perspective

### Upside Factors

1. **Local AI leadership**: Competitive advantage over global tech giants in Korean LLM
2. **Cloud growth**: Benefiting from Korean enterprise cloud migration
3. **E-commerce expansion**: AI-driven transaction growth
4. **Webtoon IP**: Expanding global OTT partnerships

### Risk Factors

1. **Global tech competition**: Google and Microsoft intensifying Korea market initiatives
2. **Regulatory risk**: Potential platform regulation strengthening (monopoly concerns, fee disputes)
3. **AI investment costs**: Potential near-term profitability pressure
4. **Advertising revenue concentration**: Vulnerability to advertising demand decline during economic slowdown

## Investment Implications

NAVER is among Korean platform companies **most aggressively pursuing AI transformation and cloud expansion**. The integration of HyperCLOVA X into search and cloud business growth are expected to serve as medium- to long-term growth drivers.

**In the near term**, increasing AI investment costs and intensifying competition from global tech giants are headwinds. However, **over the medium to long term**, rising local market demand for AI and cloud services, coupled with global expansion of webtoon IPs, are expected to support structural growth.

**Investment strategy**:
- **Medium- to long-term investors**: Cloud revenue growth trends and AI search adoption rates require monitoring
- **Short-term traders**: Pay attention to quarterly earnings announcements and cloud customer acquisition news
- **Risk management**: Monitor global tech giants' Korea market strategy developments

NAVER is transforming from a search portal into an **AI technology platform**. It has positioned itself with four major growth pillars: HyperCLOVA X search integration, cloud business growth, e-commerce GMV expansion, and global webtoon IP expansion.
