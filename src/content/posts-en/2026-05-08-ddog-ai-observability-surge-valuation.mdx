Datadog recorded its largest single-day gain on May 7, drawing attention with a Q1 2026 earnings surprise that beat market expectations. The surge reflects renewed assessment of AI infrastructure monitoring demand. However, a P/E ratio of 470x represents an extreme valuation that signals a warning for long-term investors.

## Company Overview

Datadog provides cloud-native application monitoring and observability platforms. Since its 2019 IPO, it has grown alongside AI infrastructure expansion, with a current market cap of $66.58B placing it in the mid-cap tech category. As of May 7's close, the stock price was $188.73, trading within 6% of the 52-week high of $201.69.

## Fundamentals

Datadog's gross margin of 79.96% reflects the high-margin economics characteristic of software businesses. However, its operating margin stands at -1.29%, indicating the company remains unprofitable at the operating level, with a net profit margin of only 3.14%. Return on equity is a low 3.24%, suggesting ongoing cash burn driven by heavy growth investments.

Valuation metrics are extreme. A P/E of 470x is difficult to justify at current earnings levels. Price-to-sales of 14.79x and price-to-book of 12.78x also significantly exceed industry averages. Markets are pricing in strong expectations for future profitability improvements and sustained AI demand.

## Takeaway

Datadog is being reconsidered as the invisible plumbing of AI infrastructure. Near-term momentum is strong, but valuation has already priced in excessive optimism. Investors should weigh earnings momentum against valuation gaps. At near 52-week highs, expanded volatility rather than further gains appears more likely.

Competition from cloud vendor native solutions—AWS CloudWatch, Google Cloud Monitoring—also warrants monitoring. Whether integrated platform advantages persist and whether revenue-per-customer gains translate into actual profitability improvement will be decisive.
