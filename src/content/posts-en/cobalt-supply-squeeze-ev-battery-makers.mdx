## Automakers' Cobalt Procurement Competition Pulls Mining Company Stocks Up

As electric vehicle battery demand surges, automaker moves to directly control cobalt supply chains intensify. This trend creates tailwinds for mining firms, driving global cobalt futures into prolonged strength.

## Partial Mining Company Stock Recovery, Signal of Cobalt Investment Boom

**Glencore** (**GLCNF**) trades at USD 7.74 (as of 2026-10-13) and rose 11.33% over past three months. Revenue growth reaches 48.6% year-over-year. **Vale** (**VALE**) at USD 13.22 (as of 2026-10-14) traded between USD 13.05-17.44 over recent six-month range. **Rio Tinto** (**RIO**) at USD 91.25 (as of 2026-10-14) rose 2.85% over three months with 19.6% net profit margin and 15.5% revenue growth. Mining company earnings improvement and stock volatility reflect energy transition market expectations.

## Why Cobalt Procurement Competition Intensifies: Four Drivers

**1. Battery raw material shortage worsening**: Cobalt, core battery material for EVs, has limited supply. Amid surging demand, mine development requires 3+ years lead time, making short-term supply shortage inevitable.

**2. Automaker supply chain M&A expansion**: Major automakers including Tesla, BMW, and Volkswagen directly pursue mining rights and refining facility acquisition, formalizing strategy to bypass intermediaries and stabilize costs. This increases mining firm contract demand.

**3. Mining investment resilience limits**: Significant cobalt concentration in Congo creates geopolitical risk. Advanced country governments and automakers promote development in Australia, Canada, Indonesia to diversify supply chains.

**4. Refining stage bottleneck**: Ore mining alone insufficient; refining capacity shortage creates price premium. Mining firm upstream investment profitability improving.

## Mining Companies' Positioning: Earnings and Structure Variance

**Glencore** shows fastest expansion at 48.6% revenue growth but thin margin of 1.8% net profit. **Rio Tinto** maintains most robust profit structure at 19.6% net profit margin with moderate 15.5% revenue growth. **Vale** receives high valuation at PER 26.4 but suffered -4.42% decline over recent three months undergoing correction. Given 24-36 months needed for cobalt investment returns to materialize, differences in mining company cash positions and project progress appear as stock volatility.

## Summary

Automakers' cobalt supply chain independence attempts provide medium-term growth opportunities for mining firms, yet profitability and growth speed show wide variance among companies. Process by which cobalt supply tightness leads to mining investment remains ongoing, requiring substantial time for final supply effects to reflect in market.
