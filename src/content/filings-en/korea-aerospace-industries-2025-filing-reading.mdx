## Korea Aerospace Industries, Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: Operating income rose 143% in one year and grew to 2x in another

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Korea Aerospace Industries, Ltd. (047810) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 third-quarter report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

### 1. Income statement — operating income changed by more than 70% in 2 years

| Fiscal year | Revenue | Revenue change | Operating income | Op. income change | Operating margin | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 2.83tn | — | 139.5bn | — | 4.9% | 72.3bn |

| 2022 | 2.79tn | +8.77% | 141.6bn | +143.08% | 5.1% | 115.9bn |

| 2025 | 3.70tn | +1.72% | 269.2bn | +11.83% | 7.3% | 187.3bn |

Revenue was largest in 2023 at KRW 3.82tn and smallest in 2021 at KRW 2.56tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year twice: 2021 (−9.30%), 2024 (−4.86%).

Operating income was largest in 2025 at KRW 269.2bn and smallest in 2021 at KRW 58.3bn. The operating margin was highest in 2025 at 7.3%.

**Net income was largest in 2023 at KRW 221.4bn and smallest in 2021 at KRW 53.3bn.**
