## SK Telecom Co., Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: ROE ranged between 19.6% and 2.9%

Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

Revenue was largest in 2024 at KRW 17.94tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 16.09tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year once: 2025 (−4.69%).

**Net income was largest in 2021 at KRW 2.42tn and smallest in 2025 at KRW 375.1bn.**
