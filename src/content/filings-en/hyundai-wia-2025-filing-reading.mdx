## Hyundai Wia financial statements, 6 years: Revenue rose 2 years in a row while operating income fell 2 years in a row

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Hyundai Wia (011210) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

### 1. Income statement — revenue and operating income moved in opposite directions over the last 2 years

| Fiscal year | Revenue | Revenue change | Operating income | Op. income change | Operating margin | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 6.59tn | — | 72.0bn | — | 1.1% | 53.7bn |
| 2021 | 7.53tn | +14.19% | 102.7bn | +42.76% | 1.4% | 56.1bn |
| 2022 | 8.21tn | +9.03% | 212.1bn | +106.49% | 2.6% | 43.5bn |
| 2023 | 8.17tn | −0.51% | 232.8bn | +9.75% | 2.9% | 52.5bn |
| 2024 | 8.18tn | +0.19% | 218.8bn | −6.01% | 2.7% | 129.5bn |
| 2025 | 8.48tn | +3.68% | 204.4bn | −6.58% | 2.4% | 116.9bn |

Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 8.48tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 6.59tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year once: 2023 (−0.51%).

Operating income was largest in 2023 at KRW 232.8bn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 72.0bn. The operating margin was highest in 2023 at 2.9%.

**Net income was largest in 2024 at KRW 129.5bn and smallest in 2022 at KRW 43.5bn.**
