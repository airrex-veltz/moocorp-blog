## LG Display Co., Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: Back to a profit in 2025 after a net loss of KRW 2.41tn in 2024

Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

**Net income was largest in 2021 at KRW 1.33tn and smallest in 2022 at KRW −3.20tn.**
