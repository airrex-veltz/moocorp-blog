## Nexen Corporation financial statements, 6 years: Revenue rose 5 years in a row while operating income fell 2 years in a row

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Nexen Corporation (005720) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 third-quarter report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

### 1. Income statement — revenue and operating income moved in opposite directions over the last 2 years

| Fiscal year | Revenue | Revenue change | Operating income | Op. income change | Operating margin | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 1.97tn | — | 62.3bn | — | 3.2% | 0.8bn |

Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 3.59tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 1.97tn among the six years. It never fell from the previous year.

**Net income was largest in 2025 at KRW 177.9bn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 0.8bn.**
