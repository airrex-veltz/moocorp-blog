## Samsung Biologics Co., Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: Operating income rose 84% in one year and grew to 2x in another

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Samsung Biologics Co., Ltd. (207940) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 4.56tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 1.16tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year once: 2023 (−2.08%).

**Net income was largest in 2025 at KRW 1.78tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 241.0bn.**
