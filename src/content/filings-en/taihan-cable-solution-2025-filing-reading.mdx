## Taihan Cable & Solution Co., Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: Revenue grew at an average 17.89% a year

Figures from the 2026 third-quarter report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).
