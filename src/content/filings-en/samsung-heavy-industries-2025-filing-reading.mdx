## Samsung Heavy Industries Co., Ltd. financial statements, 6 years: Back to a profit in 2024 after a net loss of KRW 155.6bn in 2023

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Samsung Heavy Industries Co., Ltd. (010140) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 10.65tn and smallest in 2022 at KRW 5.94tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year twice: 2021 (−3.47%), 2022 (−10.23%).

Operating income was largest in 2025 at KRW 862.2bn and smallest in 2021 at KRW −1.31tn. The operating margin was highest in 2025 at 8.1%.

**Net income was largest in 2025 at KRW 535.8bn and smallest in 2020 at KRW −1.49tn.**
