## Hyosung TNC Corporation financial statements, 6 years: Operating income rose 434% in one year and grew to 2x in another

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Hyosung TNC Corporation (298020) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

### 1. Income statement — operating income changed by more than 70% in 3 years

| Fiscal year | Revenue | Revenue change | Operating income | Op. income change | Operating margin | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 5.16tn | — | 266.6bn | — | 5.2% | 168.3bn |

| 2025 | 7.69tn | −1.04% | 251.5bn | −7.10% | 3.3% | 38.2bn |

Revenue was largest in 2022 at KRW 8.88tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 5.16tn among the six years. It fell from the previous year twice: 2023 (−15.26%), 2025 (−1.04%).

Operating income was largest in 2021 at KRW 1.42tn and smallest in 2022 at KRW 123.6bn. The operating margin was highest in 2021 at 16.6%.

**Net income was largest in 2021 at KRW 1.01tn and smallest in 2022 at KRW 19.2bn.**
