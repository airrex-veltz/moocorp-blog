## Hyundai Corporation financial statements, 6 years: Revenue grew at an average 21.26% a year

The consolidated financial statements in the 2020–2025 annual reports that Hyundai Corporation (011760) filed with Korea's DART electronic disclosure system, laid out by year. Figures from the 2026 half-year report cover a different period and are shown separately. **Every figure below is taken as filed in the DART submissions; there is no interpretation or outlook.** Amounts are in Korean won (KRW; bn = billion, tn = trillion).

### 1. Income statement — the compound annual growth rate of revenue was 21.26%

| Fiscal year | Revenue | Revenue change | Operating income | Op. income change | Operating margin | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 2.88tn | — | 33.2bn | — | 1.1% | 49.4bn |
| 2021 | 3.78tn | +31.30% | 35.1bn | +5.51% | 0.9% | 38.0bn |
| 2022 | 6.13tn | +61.98% | 66.8bn | +90.56% | 1.1% | 78.7bn |
| 2023 | 6.58tn | +7.40% | 99.3bn | +48.64% | 1.5% | 83.4bn |
| 2024 | 7.00tn | +6.31% | 133.5bn | +34.43% | 1.9% | 121.0bn |
| 2025 | 7.55tn | +7.99% | 140.1bn | +4.94% | 1.9% | 86.8bn |

Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 7.55tn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 2.88tn among the six years. It never fell from the previous year.

Operating income was largest in 2025 at KRW 140.1bn and smallest in 2020 at KRW 33.2bn. The operating margin was highest in 2024 at 1.9%.

**Net income was largest in 2024 at KRW 121.0bn and smallest in 2021 at KRW 38.0bn.**

The current ratio was below 100% in 1 year, 2023 — in those years current liabilities exceeded current assets.
